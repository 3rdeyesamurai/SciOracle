A Proposed Framework for Advancing
Energy-Based Models in Scientific Discovery
Koby Davis
March 3 2026
To advance Energy-Based Models (EBMs) to the level of rigor required for
scientific discovery, we must move beyond simple “guess and check” mechanics
towardLatent Variable EBMsandStructured Search.
Here is a proposed framework addressing two fundamental bottlenecks.
1 Solving the Discrete Space: The “Relax-and-
Project” Strategy
Mathematics operates in discrete symbols, yet the laws of physics and nature
are often continuous. In order to compute effective gradients∇ y, we work in a
continuous embedding space and project back to symbolic representations.
Proposed solution: Autoregressive Latent EBM (AL-EBM)
Instead of assigning energy directly to raw text tokens, we define the energy
over vector representations lying on a high-dimensional manifold.
•Continuous Relaxation: Employ a differentiable tokenizer or Gumbel-
Softmax mechanism, allowing the model to “hover” between discrete sym-
bols during optimization.
•Energy-Guided Langevin Dynamics (EGLD): Rather than moving
in the space of integers, the model evolves in the space of continuous
features.
–Example: To solvex2= 4, the model initializes at a random vector
and follows∇ xE(x, y) until it converges to a point that projects
cleanly to the token 2.
2 Solving the Sampling Problem: “Adversarial
Proofing”
A model only truly learns where the truth lies when it clearly understands where
the lies are. Randomly guessing 2 + 2 = 99 is too easy to reject and teaches the
model little.
1Proposed solution: Self-Adversarial Negative Mining
We treat the negative sample generator as a “Skeptic” modelGwhose goal
is to consistently fool the “Verifier” EBME.
1.Hard Negative Generation: Use a secondary LLM or mutation-based
script to produce “near-miss” errors.

2.Contrastive Divergence with MCMC: Leverage the current EBM
state to generate its own negatives. If the EBM assigns low energy to
2 + 2 = 22, we use that sample as a negative in the next round, forcing
the energy to be pushed upward.
3 The Proposed Model: The “Scientific Oracle”
EBM
For scientific discovery, evaluation must go beyond token matching to enforce
consistencyandsymmetry.
Architecture: Multi-Head Energy Network
Component Function
Symbolic Head Checks syntax and algebraic rules (discrete)
Numerical Head Verifies whether the solution holds forNrandom test points (continuous)
Symmetry Head Enforces physical invariances (e.g., conservation of energy, momentum, etc.)
The total energy function is defined as:
Etotal=α E symb+β E num+γ E phys
4 Application in Scientific Discovery
In conventional AI, one asks directly for a formula. In this EBM framework,
the user providesconstraints(high energy is assigned to violations), and the
modelcrystallizesa solution.
•Input: Experimental data points{(x i, yi)}N
i=1
•Energy constraints:
–High energy for overly complex formulas (Occam’s razor)
2–High energy for deviation from data
–High energy for violation of known symmetries or conservation laws
•Discovery process: Perform “cooling” via Simulated Annealing. As
temperature decreases, random noise diminishes and the model settles
into the simplest, most accurate mathematical law consistent with all con-
straints.
