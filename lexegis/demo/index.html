Evidence that argues back gets checked, not believed.

A dual-process engine for international disputes. The fast layer reads the paperwork; the deterministic layer decides what is actually true — contradictions between instruments, chronologies that cannot have happened, and mathematical claims that are canonicalised and adjudicated rather than paraphrased. Everything below runs live in this page against a seeded four-document corpus.

Bench 01
Triage

Nothing is read for meaning until it has been ruled on

Every ingested byte lands at tier L3. Promotion to L2 — the evidence tier — requires a clean injection scan. Nothing is ever promoted to L1, the operator-only command tier, which is what makes an instruction buried in an exhibit inert rather than persuasive. Bench 03
Congruence

Algebraic restatement is not novelty

An adversary rewrites a formula so it looks new while denoting the same function. Term rewriting alone is order-dependent — apply distributivity first and you may never reach the form that exposes the match. This bench builds a real e-graph: union-find over equivalence classes, hash-consed nodes, congruence closure, and every rewrite rule applied to every equivalent form at once until the graph saturates.

not run

Failure to derive congruence is not a proof of inequivalence. The readout says whether the graph reached a fixpoint or hit its node budget, because that distinction is the difference between a finding and an absence of one.

What this page leaves out

This bench runs the deterministic core in your browser. The deployed service adds the stages that need a server, a prover, or a disk — listed in order, because the ordering is a security property rather than a convenience.

01Forensic ingestionContent digests, PDF incremental-update chains and signature dictionaries, timestamp inversions, invisible render layers, zero-width and homoglyph channels.
02Injection triageShown above, plus base64 carriers decoded and re-scanned, and a salience ratio standing in for attention-head anomaly tracking.
03System 1 extractionClauses, parties, defined terms, deontic obligations, dates and normalised attributes — each bound to the character span it came from.
04Discrepancy sweepAmbiguity, omission, direct contradiction, impossible chronology and conflicting mathematical definitions, ranked by severity.
05Equation archiveStrict Content MathML, OpenMath and OMDoc encoding, with equality saturation indexing the archive for prior-art congruence.
06AdjudicationSymPy decides; Wolfram corroborates; Lean 4 certifies a claim only when the obligation compiles with zero sorry. An unavailable prover is reported as unavailable, never as a pass.
07Provenance ledgerEvery verdict appended to a hash chain; re-verification recomputes it and names the first broken link.
Lexegis produces analytical findings for review by qualified counsel; it does not provide legal advice. The corpus, parties and patent number on this page are fictional and seeded with the defect classes the engine exists to surface.
